## 🔧 Essential Portal Settings

Quick reference for Portal setup:

```
Respawn:           DISABLED
Round Limit:       11 (Suppression) / 7 (Extraction) / 13 (Demo)
Round Time:        180s / 240s / 150s
Team Size:         6v6 (recommended)
Vehicles:          DISABLED
Health Regen:      Delayed (5 seconds)
Friendly Fire:     Optional
```

## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Start Simple:** Begin with Suppression mode on Kaleidoscope
2. **Test Thoroughly:** Try your config with friends before going public
3. **Communicate:** Make rules clear to players
4. **Iterate:** Adjust settings based on feedback
5. **Have Fun:** SOCOM is about tactical teamwork and enjoyment
