# SOCOM Weapon Loadouts

## Overview
Weapon loadouts are designed to recreate the tactical, balanced gameplay of classic SOCOM. These restrictions ensure fair, skill-based combat that emphasizes precision over spray-and-pray tactics.

## General Weapon Rules
- No automatic shotguns
- No rocket launchers or explosives (except Demolition mode)
- No sniper rifles with thermal scopes
- Limited grenade inventory
- No vehicle weapons

## Primary Weapons

### Assault Rifles
**Allowed:**
- M5A3 (M4A1 equivalent)
- AK-24 (AK-47 equivalent)
- SFAR-M GL (with underslung grenade launcher for Demolition)
- AC-42

**Restrictions:**
- Single fire or burst mode preferred
- No high-capacity magazines (max 30 rounds)
- Standard optics only (Red dot, Holographic, ACOG)

### Submachine Guns
**Allowed:**
- MP9
- PP-29
- PBX-45
- K30

**Restrictions:**
- Maximum 50-round magazines
- Close to medium range optics only

### Designated Marksman Rifles
**Allowed:**
- DM7
- VCAR
- SVK

**Restrictions:**
- Maximum 4x optic zoom
- No bipods
- Limited to 1-2 per team

### Shotguns
**Allowed:**
- MCS-880 (pump action only)

**Restrictions:**
- Pump action only, no auto shotguns
- Standard buckshot rounds
- Limited to 1 per team

## Secondary Weapons

### Pistols
**Allowed:**
- M44 (revolver)
- MP28 (machine pistol)
- G57 (service pistol)

**Restrictions:**
- Standard magazines only
- Basic optics or iron sights

## Equipment

### Lethal
**Allowed:**
- Frag Grenade (maximum 1)
- C5 Explosive (Demolition mode only)
- Smoke Grenade

**Restrictions:**
- Maximum 1 lethal grenade per player
- C5 only for bomb carrier in Demolition mode

### Tactical
**Allowed:**
- Medical Crate
- Ammo Crate
- Insertion Beacon (disabled)
- Smoke Grenade Launcher

**Restrictions:**
- No spawn beacons
- No motion sensors
- Limited resupply capabilities

## Specialist Restrictions
- Disable all specialist abilities OR
- Use uniform soldier loadouts only
- No wingsuits or grappling hooks
- No automated turrets or robots

## Portal Configuration

### Weapon Restrictions Setup
```
1. Open Portal Rules Editor
2. Navigate to Weapon Restrictions
3. Disable all vehicles
4. Set allowed weapon list
5. Limit attachments and optics
6. Set magazine capacity limits
7. Disable specialist gadgets
8. Configure grenade limits
```

### Recommended Settings
```
Starting Ammo: Full
Ammo Reserves: Standard (not extended)
Grenade Cooldown: 60 seconds
Health Regeneration: Delayed (5 seconds)
Movement Speed: Standard
Sprint Duration: Standard
```

## Balancing Notes
- Assault rifles should be the primary choice
- SMGs for aggressive players and close quarters
- DMRs limited per team to prevent camping
- Shotguns as specialist close-range option
- Equipment focused on teamwork (smoke, ammo, medical)

## Tips for Implementation
1. Test weapon balance in private matches
2. Adjust restrictions based on gameplay feedback
3. Consider separate loadouts for different game modes
4. Use Portal's loadout editor to create presets
5. Lock loadouts at round start to prevent switching
6. Disable weapon pickups from downed enemies (optional)
